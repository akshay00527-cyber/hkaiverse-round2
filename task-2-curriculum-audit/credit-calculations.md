## 6. Calculating the Proposed Curriculum Change

Once the official programme-credit total is verified, calculate the percentage of programme credits affected by the proposed redesign.

**Formula:**

Percentage affected = (Distinct credits affected by proposed changes ÷ Verified total programme credits) × 100

The assignment target is approximately **20–25% of programme credits**.

Count each affected course only once, even if it appears under multiple recommendations. Explain whether a course is being updated, reduced, replaced, or newly introduced, and identify any prerequisite or regulatory restrictions.

## 7. Credit-Balance and Prerequisite Checks

Before approving the final proposal, check that:

- The revised programme total matches the required official total, unless the applicable regulations explicitly permit a change.
- Mandatory courses and required credits remain covered.
- Prerequisites are preserved for advanced subjects.
- Proposed additions do not create timetable or semester-load conflicts.
- Reduced or replaced content does not remove required learning outcomes.
- Internship, project, and assessment requirements remain compliant with the applicable regulations.

## 8. Evidence and Limitations

The Semester I–VI figures are based on student academic portal screenshots supplied for this audit. The course details for Semesters IV and V were transcribed from screenshots showing their course codes, titles, and units.

This is preliminary working evidence, not a certified academic transcript or an independently verified curriculum document. The official curriculum version, credit equivalence, and missing Semester VII–VIII details must be verified before the final credit-impact calculation is submitted.

No claim is made that the proposed curriculum changes have already been approved by the institution.
