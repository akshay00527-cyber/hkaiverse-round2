# CampusFlow — Time-Saving Calculations

## 1. Purpose

This calculation estimates the possible time impact of using CampusFlow for college requests. The figures below are illustrative assumptions because the existing college workflow has not yet been observed or measured.

## 2. Assumptions

| Activity | Estimated time per request |
|---|---:|
| Current manual handling | 12 minutes |
| Proposed handling using CampusFlow | 7 minutes |
| Additional review and support | 2 minutes |

These estimates must be validated through permitted observation or voluntary feedback.

## 3. Calculation

**Net time saved per request**

Net time saved = Current handling time − Proposed handling time − Additional review/support time

= 12 − 7 − 2

= **3 minutes per request**

**Illustrative monthly impact**

Assuming 100 requests per month:

3 minutes × 100 requests = 300 minutes

300 ÷ 60 = **5 hours saved per month**

## 4. Interpretation

Under these assumptions, CampusFlow could save approximately 3 minutes per request, or 5 hours per month for 100 requests.

These results are hypothetical. They do not establish actual savings at the selected college.

## 5. Limitations

- The current handling time has not been measured.
- The proposed handling time has not been measured across a representative sample.
- Monthly request volume is assumed to be 100.
- Setup, training, maintenance, and exceptional cases may change the results.
- A fair comparison requires measuring the existing and proposed workflows using the same definitions and comparable requests.

## 6. Validation Plan

With permission, record the handling time for a small sample of requests in the existing workflow and the prototype. Include review and support time, document the sample size, and calculate the average for each workflow.

Replace the illustrative assumptions with measured values when reliable evidence becomes available.
## Illustrative Time-Saving Calculation

### Purpose

This calculation estimates the potential time difference between handling a request through email and handling it through the CampusFlow prototype. The figures below are hypothetical planning assumptions, not measurements of actual college staff performance.

### Assumptions per request

| Activity | Illustrative time |
|---|---:|
| Existing email-based workflow | 12 minutes |
| Proposed CampusFlow workflow | 7 minutes |
| Additional review and support time for CampusFlow | 2 minutes |

### Calculation

Gross time saved per request:

12 − 7 = 5 minutes

Net time saved per request after additional review and support:

12 − 7 − 2 = 3 minutes

For an illustrative workload of 100 requests per month:

3 × 100 = 300 minutes

300 ÷ 60 = 5 hours per month

### Interpretation and limitations

Under these assumptions, CampusFlow could save approximately 3 minutes per request, or 5 hours per month for 100 requests.

These figures are illustrative only. The existing email-handling time, proposed handling time, additional support time, and monthly request volume have not been measured. The calculation does not demonstrate actual savings. A valid evaluation would measure the existing workflow and test the proposed workflow using comparable requests, while including setup, training, maintenance, and review effort where relevant.
